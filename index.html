GOOD TO KNOW

Questions?
We've got you.

Not sure what to book? That's what the consultation is for.

Which facial should I book? +

If you're unsure, start with the Signature Facial or reach out before booking. We'll choose a treatment based on your skin goals and current routine.

Can I add a massage? +

Relaxing facial massage is included in facial experiences as appropriate. Neck, shoulder, and scalp massage may be available as add-ons.

Are chemical peels right for me? +

Peels aren't right for every skin type or situation. A consultation helps determine suitability, preparation, and aftercare.

What should I do before my appointment? +

Arrive with your skin goals and current skincare routine in mind. Share any sensitivities, medications, recent treatments, or skin concerns with your esthetician.
